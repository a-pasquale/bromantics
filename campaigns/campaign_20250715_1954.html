It’s a day of music, food, swimming, and good vibes at the river!Enjoy summer with great music from The Bromantics, Mass Resistor, Cookie Club, Everdeen, and more bands to be announced. Other logistics: All ages welcome! We’ll be outside, there are a good number of chairs, but bring your own if it’s easy. We recommend bug spray. Please leave dogs at home.
