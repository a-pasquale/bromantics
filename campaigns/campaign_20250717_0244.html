Back to the River

Friday, July 18, 2025 • 3:00 - 8:30 PM

📍 49 Aqua Vitae Rd, Hadley

📅 Add to Calendar

All ages

It’s a day of music, food, swimming, and good vibes at the river!

Enjoy summer with great music from The Bromantics, Mass Resistor, Cookie Club, Everdeen, and more bands to be announced.

Bring your swimsuit, towel, and water toys and have fun in the water. There is a nice swimming spot along the Connecticut River.

Let’s have dinner together. Bring food or a beverage to share, a plate, utensils, and a cup. There will be a grill and plenty of ice. Don't have anything to contribute? No problem, we'll have plenty to share.

Other logistics: All ages welcome! We’ll be outside, there are a good number of chairs, but bring your own if it’s easy. We recommend bug spray. Please leave dogs at home.
